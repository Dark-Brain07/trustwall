#### TrustWallRegistry & TrustWallEscrow

**Title:** TrustWall: AI-Adjudicated Escrow Network for Freelancers
**Description:**
TrustWall is a robust, self-contained factory registry and escrow protocol designed for freelance milestones. It utilizes GenLayer's AI Equivalence Principle by passing work specifications and submitted evidence through non-deterministic LLM validation (handling network/URL errors gracefully). The LLMs reach a unified consensus on whether the evidence meets the specification (`meets_spec` boolean) and dynamically trigger automated payouts or revision cycles.
